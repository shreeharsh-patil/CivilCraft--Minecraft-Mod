# CivilCraft AI - Core Vision & Gameplay Pillars

## 🌌 The Vision
**CivilCraft AI** brings the deep strategic gameplay of 4X games (specifically Sid Meier's *Civilization*) into the first-person voxel survival world of Minecraft. Unlike typical multiplayer servers that rely on custom plugins and text chats, CivilCraft AI uses **fully autonomous, LLM-powered AI agents** that inhabit the world as villagers, town leaders, traders, and soldiers.

These agents can build, farm, trade, plan, write letters, establish treaties, and hold voice/text conversation with players in real-time, adapting dynamically to the geopolitical landscape of the world.

---

## 🏛️ Gameplay Pillars

### 1. Survival Nation Building
Players do not just build houses; they establish **Settlements** by building a *Town Hall*. Town boundaries expand as culture and population grow.
*   **Infrastructure**: Houses, farms, barracks, mines, and markets are built by players and AI villagers working together.
*   **Upkeep**: Settlements require food and tax revenue to maintain their boundaries and prevent decay.

### 2. LLM-Powered Agent Geopolitics
AI Agents act as leaders of foreign factions, mayors of town councils, or specialized citizens.
*   **Autonomous Decisions**: Factions expand their cities, claim resource nodes, and build military units.
*   **Natural Dialogue**: Players can chat with agents to negotiate trade deals, sign peace treaties, or receive quests.
*   **Persistent Memories**: Agents remember player actions (e.g., helping them build a library, or killing a citizen) and adjust their relationships accordingly.

### 3. Deep Economy & Trade
*   **Currency**: A unified copper, silver, and gold coin system backed by the SQLite database ledger.
*   **Trade Routes**: Factions set up trade routes via merchants. Protecting these caravans from bandits (or raiding them) is a core strategic element.
*   **Resource Nodes**: Strategic resources (coal veins, iron deposits, fertile plains, diamond chasms) must be claimed to unlock building structures or advanced tech.

### 4. Dynamic Technology Tree
*   **Research Points (RP)**: Earned through building libraries, generating wealth, mining, or defeating enemies.
*   **Ages**: Progress from the *Stone Age* to the *Steam/Redstone Age*, unlocking new block registries, stronger tools, and automation machinery.
